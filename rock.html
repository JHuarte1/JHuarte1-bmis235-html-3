Rock Creek Cattle Company

This golf course is located in Deer Lodge, Montana.

On the Rocky mountain foothills, Rock Creek provides a stunning golf environment on a working cattle ranch. The course plays through pines and across Rock creek. This course is ranked #1 in the state of Montana, as it provides one of the best golf experiences in the entire country.
